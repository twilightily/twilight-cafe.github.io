# Hi! I'm Ananya Abhilash <3

<br>

<p>
            Also known as twilight! I see tech as a form of art<br>
            and well anyone that knows me, knows that I LOVE art (checkout the art tab for more)<br>
            I'm passionate about cool things. Learning new stuff is always something I enjoy, things like understanding what goes on behind the scenes in communication from process to process (goat networks class omg) or how interactive sites update their view based on actions by the user 
        </p>

<br>

I'm currently a second year CSE student at [PES University](pes.edu). I'm part of two awesome tech clubs at uni i.e. [ACM PESU ECC](https://pesuecc.acm.org/Website-2026/) Branch and HSP. I'm infact the Design Lead in HSP'27 core! 
[HSP](https://hsp-ec.xyz/) which was once just a college club has since then grown into [Homebrew](https://homebrew.hsp-ec.xyz/), a FOSS community open to everyone! My core team and I have thus far put a lot of effort into getting this community up and I must say it is incredibly fun watching it grow.

<br>

So far most of my work has been a really nice blend of both design and tech. For example, I built and deployed my first web app on Streamlit called [Wordle Unhinged](https://github.com/twilightily/wordle-unhinged), a fun twist to the classic Wordle. I also was a mentee at 
Tilde 5.0 (a summer mentorship program conducted by Homebrew) where I built [MuSSHroom](https://github.com/homebrew-foss/muSSHroom), an SSH chat server written in Go with a VERY pretty TUI created using [BubbleTea](https://github.com/charmbracelet/bubbletea) framework 
Right now, I'm into exploring networks, I'm creating small things here and there like a network diagnosis tool written in python which can be tested on a mininet topology or coding theory with a linear algebra application of how integrity check is done with parity matrices. I'm currently starting to create my own netstack purely for fun xd

<br>

Art is something that has been with me since the beginning of time. Ofcourse I was a traditional artist at first working with poster paints, colour pencils and JUST pencils xD however around lockdown time, I dabbled into digital art! I started out on IbisPaintX on literally my phone and my finger (no stylus). Overtime I built my expertise in drawing onscreen and it has been quite the journey
Take a look at all my art and design work in the Art&Design tab :D

<br>

I've also done quite a lot of design work for HSP and ACM, mainly event posters spawned on canva/figma with backgrounds created from scratch by me on IbisPaintX

<br>

One thing that's for sure though, I'm very much against using AI in any of the work I do (ESPECIALLY creative work). It is indeed a helpful tool, however I feel like it takes the joy of learning something new out of all our daily lives. I truly believe in using it as a tool to learn rather than a tool to do everything for you
If you keep escaping things what are you escaping for?
(might I add, the posters designed by AI make me nauseous because :p)
